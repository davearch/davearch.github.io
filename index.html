Hi there!

 

Web Developer, Programmer, System Administrator

Institute of the Environment, University of Arizona

I'm a 23 year old IT person, currently working as a web developer at the Institute of the Environment. Besides working mostly front end duties on the many Drupal sites we run, most of my time is spent in a Linux box completing various sysadmin tasks.

I have provisioned several KVM hypervisors, configured our local rig with puppet, continue to manage logs with a logstash server (ELK stack), as well as maintain regular backups with a bacula setup. Our custom themes are under git version control, and our development workflow is expedited with on-the-fly docker containers (on a VM that I created and maintain). I have also setup multi-factor authentication with yubikeys for some of our most important servers, such as an internal file sharing server (another project of mine) that runs both the AFP and SAMBA protocols (to serve both our Windows and OS X users), and on a hypervisor I setup for some folks in the Physics and Atmospheric Sciences Department here at the U of A.

I enjoy Linux, and programming in general. I write a lot of scripts and contribute to open source software. In my spare time I like to hike, climb, and bike around the beautiful city of Tucson.
